# 05 – Picture in picture

## US-5.1 Place the camera — Done
As a creator, I want to drag the camera anywhere on the screen recording.
- Drag to move; the camera cannot leave the frame.
- Four buttons snap it to a corner with a small margin.
- "Reset to Reframed layout" restores the position and size saved by Reframed.

## US-5.2 Resize the camera — Done
As a creator, I want to change the camera size by dragging its corner handle or with a slider.

## US-5.3 Round camera — Done
As a creator, I want the camera clipped to a circle instead of a rectangle.
- Rectangle / Circle switch; the circle is exported as a real circle with a smooth edge.

## US-5.4 Rounded corners — Done
As a creator, I want rounded corners on the rectangular camera.
- Corner radius slider; the radius is exported.

## US-5.5 Choose what the camera shows — Done
As a creator, I want to zoom into the camera image and move the visible area, so that my face is framed well.
- Zoom 1× to 4× and left/right, up/down position, for both circle and rectangle.
- Preview and export use the same crop.

## US-5.6 Mirror the camera — Done
As a creator, I want to flip the camera horizontally.

## US-5.7 Border and shadow — Planned
As a creator, I want a border and a drop shadow around the camera in the exported video (the preview shows a shadow, the export does not).

## US-5.8 Camera layout changes over time — Planned
As a creator, I want the camera to be full screen, hidden or moved for parts of the video.
